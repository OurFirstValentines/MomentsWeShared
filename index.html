To You, Samara

My Dearest Samara,

Words could never fully capture what you mean to me, but I'll try anyway. You are my favorite notification, my best good morning text, and the reason I believe in love stories.

Every moment with you feels like a gift I didn't know I could receive. Your laugh is my favorite sound, your smile my favorite sight, and your presence my favorite feeling.

Thank you for being you — for your patience, your kindness, your incredible spirit. Thank you for choosing me, for loving me, for making every ordinary day extraordinary.

This website is just a small token of my love for you. A digital scrapbook of us, our memories, our journey together. I can't wait to add more photos, more moments, more chapters to our story.

Happy Valentine's Day, my love. Today and always, you have my heart.

Forever Yours 💛
